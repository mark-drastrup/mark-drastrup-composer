# Composer Website

The public portfolio site for Mark Drastrup's music: showcasing finished work to prospective clients and growing a newsletter audience.

## Language

**Track**:
A finished piece of music published on the site, with a title and an audio recording.
_Avoid_: Song, tune, piece

**Showcase**:
The curated, ordered selection of Tracks featured on the home page. A Track can exist without being in the Showcase.
_Avoid_: Song list, playlist, portfolio

**Lead Magnet**:
A free downloadable offered in exchange for a newsletter signup, such as the Gameboy track MIDI. Distinct from a Track in the Showcase, even when it contains music.
_Avoid_: Freebie, download
